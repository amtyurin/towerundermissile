# Missile Attack Free: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Missile Attack Free ("the app") is a game in which you defend a city from falling missiles. It is published by Andrey Tyurin.

## Data the app stores

The app stores only these game settings and results:

- the game speed and game style you selected
- whether sound is on or off
- your three high scores (one for each game speed)

They are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app uses them only to restore your settings and to show your high scores.

## Data the app does not collect or share

- The app does not send any data off your device. It has no internet permission.
- The app does not share data with third parties.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.
- The app does not use Android backup, so its settings and high scores are not copied to cloud storage.
- The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, account or any other personal information.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app to delete all of its data. You can also clear the app's storage in your device's Settings app.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
